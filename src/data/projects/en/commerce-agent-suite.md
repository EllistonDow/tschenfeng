## System Overview

OmniCommerce AI translates AI Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) principles into operational leverage for high-volume cross-border DTC brands and commerce enterprises.

### Core Subsystems
- **Global Market Intelligence Radar**: Scrapes search trends, consumer reviews, and competitive pricing across global marketplaces to generate actionable product opportunities.
- **Programmatic Catalog Engine**: Interfaces directly with the Shopify Admin GraphQL API to execute automated copywriting, imagery enhancement, and multi-market SEO localization.
- **Autonomous Support Orchestration**: Provides 24/7 customer service across European and North American timezones, managing order tracking, refund pre-validation, and returns routing.
